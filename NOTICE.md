# Third-party material

The MIT license in this repository covers the code only.

`index.html` embeds a few pieces of art and short clips as decoration. They were supplied by the project author from elsewhere on the internet, belong to their original creators, and are included here as unofficial fan decoration:

- A chibi sticker of Omen from VALORANT (Riot Games).
- A pixel Kirby clip (Kirby is a Nintendo character).
- A pixel duck clip, a cat-with-sword-and-shield meme clip, and a white-cat meme clip. Original creators unknown.
- Three hand-drawn sun illustrations. Original artists unknown.

If you are a rights holder and want something removed, open an issue and it will be taken out promptly. If you fork this project for anything beyond personal or portfolio use, replace these with your own art.

The fonts Bagel Fat One, Bricolage Grotesque and Permanent Marker are loaded from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.
